Privacy policy

What Outlier stores, why, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

What Outlier collects

Browsing Outlier needs no account and collects no personal information. If you save products, the list stays in your browser's local storage on your device.

If you choose to sign in, we store the following so your saved list can follow you to other devices:

  • Your email address, from the email link or from your Google account.
  • Your basic Google profile (name and picture) if you sign in with Google. Outlier only asks for your email and basic profile.
  • The products you have saved: store, product link, name, image and the price when you saved it.

How it is used

Only to sign you in and to keep your saved list in sync. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles. Outlier does not run advertising or analytics trackers.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in) and, for email sign-in links, Resend (email delivery). Google handles Google sign-in under its own privacy policy. Each person's saved list is protected so that only that signed-in person can read or change it.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time. Signing out removes the saved list from that device.
  • Ask us to delete your account and saved list by emailing admin@adaptbranding.ca from the address you signed in with.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove anything stored on this device.

Contact

Questions about this policy: admin@adaptbranding.ca.
