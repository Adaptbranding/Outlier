About saved products

Saved products live in this browser's local storage on this device. Outlier has no accounts, so the list does not follow you to another phone or computer, and clearing your browser's site data or using a private window removes it. Each saved product keeps the photo, link and price from the moment you saved it. The "now" price comes from the latest daily scan of that store; if a product is missing from the scan it is no longer 50% off or more, or it sold out, so open the store to check. Store prices and stock can differ from what was scanned.
